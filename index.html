EscrowDispute

AI-arbitrated escrow for marketplace disputes. GenLayer validators judge deliverables against the agreed spec and settle funds automatically — no human moderator required.

GenLayer Agent Tank Hackathon · Onchain Justice

THE DISPUTE PROBLEM

Ordinary smart contracts can verify a signature or a balance. They cannot tell whether a freelance logo, a piece of writing, or any creative deliverable actually satisfies what was agreed. That judgment call is usually left to a platform, a support ticket, or a slow human arbitrator.

EscrowDispute puts that judgment onchain instead — resolved by consensus among independent AI validators, not a single party with a thumb on the scale.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. Buyer opens the case

    Deploys the contract with the seller's address and a plain-language spec — e.g. "Design a vector logo containing a blue dragon."

  2. Seller submits the deliverable

    Calls submit_work with a link or description of the completed work.

  3. The AI jury is convened

    Calling resolve_dispute triggers independent GenLayer validators to each evaluate the deliverable against the spec, then reach consensus through GenLayer's Equivalence Principle.

  4. Verdict settles automatically

    The contract updates its own status and the outcome is final — no appeal panel, no support queue.

RELEASED_TO_SELLER

Deliverable satisfies the spec. Funds move to the seller.

REFUNDED_TO_BUYER

Deliverable does not satisfy the spec. Funds return to the buyer.
